# superagent: decision cards

Status: agreed with the owner on 2026-11-09; C7 added on 2026-11-10.

## Context

The owner wants every decision a team needs from them in one place, in DSH's right bar, each with a recommended option, and to approve the routine ones with one click.

## Decisions

- **C1 A card has options and a recommendation.** A card is a question, two to six options, the option its proposer recommends, and a level from P0 to P3. A card without a recommendation among its options is refused. A card with no level counts as P1.
- **C2 Who raises one.** The router raises a card for any recommendation it has for the owner (`superagent_decision_propose`), and does not give it as prose. An observer can raise one (`a2a_decision_propose`). A member's question on a ticket must be a card: `a2a_ticket_report` refuses a question without options and a recommendation.
- **C3 How one is answered.** P2 and P3 can be approved with one click, which takes the recommended option. P0 and P1 cannot: the owner picks an option and confirms. The host enforces this, not only the page. The owner can also pick another option or write their own decision on any card.
- **C4 Every decision becomes the team's work.** A card raised by the router or an observer becomes, once decided, a request from the owner on a new thread. It says who raised the card, so the proposer's words are not read as the owner's own. It reaches the router as a candidate and is accepted and dispatched as any request. A member's card belongs to the ticket it was asked on, which is already the team's work: the decision goes back to that ticket. Opening a second ticket would leave the first waiting for an answer.
- **C5 Only the owner decides.** Deciding needs the host's GUI login, as rule edits do (router-owner-asks D10), so a member's shell cannot approve its own card. The host admits the original incoming request and it must name the authenticated browser-owner Peer. Domain/forwarded-port logins are not replayed against loopback. An unavailable/older Host Connection service fails closed with `owner-auth-unavailable`; a control-route key or an authenticated API Peer does not replace the browser owner's confirmation (owner-approved repair, 2026-11-14).
- **C6 A loop cannot bury the owner.** One proposer has at most ten cards open, and an identical open question returns the existing card.
- **C7 Decided cards are a record, not an archive.** A decided card is kept 30 days, and at most the newest 500. A member's card whose ticket has ended is dropped. A card still waiting for the owner is never dropped.
- **C8 Files on a card (owner 2026-11-10).** The router and an observer raising a card, and a member asking a question, may put up to 16 files from their workspace on it (`files`, paths). The host stages them on the file channel granted to the team's canvas, as `a2a_file_send` does, and the card lists them; the owner downloads one through `GET /__dsh_a2a/superagent/decisions/file`, which serves only a file a card names, behind the same owner proof as deciding. The owner may attach files when deciding: the right bar stages them for the card's team like a request's files, and their reference lines go below the decision to the router or the ticket's member, who pull them with `a2a_file_fetch`. The file channel keeps a file 24 hours; after that the card still lists it, and the download says it has expired.
- **C9 A waiting card is never hidden (feedback 2026-11-12).** The right bar's team view shows the decisions whether or not its team list can be read or has a team to send to; those only change the request box. Only a read that returns a `decisions` array counts as the list. A refused or failed read says so and keeps the last cards it read, marked as possibly out of date, until a later poll succeeds. Nothing there decides, retries a send or changes a card.

## Levels

The proposer sets the level:

- P0: irreversible, or touches production or other people's data.
- P1: significant and costly to undo.
- P2: routine, easy to undo.
- P3: trivial.

## Consequences

A proposer can mark a card lower than it deserves. The owner still sees the question and the recommendation before clicking, so this costs attention, not authority. Prose advice from the router cannot be blocked by the host; its persona requires the card.

- **T8 step 1 — the market shows the router's and observer's cards (2026-11-20; envelope v1 settled with the market, workbench-market #898).** Off unless the owner turns on the team setting `decisionPush` (a rule change, so it needs the owner's proof; off, nothing is queued, written or sent — turn it on only once the market runs the #898 receiver, since an older market's `/a2a/direct` would take the envelope as an ordinary message). While it is on and a router or observer card is raised, decided or withdrawn, the host queues one event and sends it on the roster's route (P3): a signed direct message from the host's process face to the canvas remote members that declare `teamRoster`. The text's first line is exactly `[A2A decision-card] v1`, then one JSON object: `event` (created / decided / dropped), `rev` (per card, only grows: the larger of the stored revision plus one and the clock in milliseconds, so a lost or broken state file never sends it backwards), `decide_at` (`/__dsh_a2a_nexus/?tab=team&view=decisions&card=<encodeURIComponent(id)>`), and `card` (id, team = the roster's `<zone>/canvas/<name>`, proposer_kind, level, question, options, recommended, reason, files as name/size/sha256 only, created_at, and on decided decided_at and decided_option — null when the owner decided in words only). The sender checks the market's limits field by field: a reason over 2000 characters or a file name over 200 is cut (marked with …) and sent; anything else the market would refuse (32 KB body, id rule, 2–6 distinct non-empty options of at most 300 characters, recommended among them, level P0–P3, a non-empty question of at most 2000 characters, 16 files with a 64-hex sha256, times that parse, a decided event with its decision) stops the card here. A transport failure keeps the event for the next 10-second tick; a refusal that would repeat (400, 401, 403, 429, read from the status code when the error carries one) drops it. Either way a stopped or refused card is logged and becomes the health problem `decision-push-refused`, one per team and card, which goes to the observer first (R7a); it clears when a later event of that card goes through or the host no longer holds the card. The sender compares options, the recommended option and the question as the market cleans them (control characters to spaces, direction controls dropped, ends trimmed), and a cut never splits an emoji. Turning `decisionPush` off drops the waiting events and the refused-card problems (revisions stay), so turning it on again sends only what happens from then on. A card already on the market whose events fell in the off period may then stay as the market last saw it: the host's own view is the one that counts. Known limits: an event the transport keeps failing holds up the ones behind it until the 200-event outbox drops the oldest; with more than one market, an event leaves the outbox once any of them takes it. Members' ticket questions and the host's own cards (health, tool, outbound, staffing, sender) never go. Deciding stays here, by the owner logged in in the browser: C5 is unchanged. The market sees a card only after it has the host's roster from the same key.
- **T8 step 2 — the market's link lands on the card (2026-11-20).** `decide_at` opens the A2A workbench at `?tab=team&view=decisions&card=<id>`. The workbench reads only those three parameters, takes the card id only when it fits the card id rule, removes them from the address bar, and opens 团队 › 待你处理 at that card, scrolled to and flashed; a card no longer waiting there says it was handled or is not on this machine. 待你处理 carries a 等你拍板 group with the router's and the observer's waiting cards of this instance's teams (the cards the host pushes to the market; a member's question is answered on its ticket card, so it is neither shown nor counted twice), decided as in the DSH right bar through the same route (`/__dsh_a2a/superagent/decisions`) — so the owner can decide in either place. Whether the person is the owner is the host's call alone (browser owner proof, C5): the workbench shows every button and reports the host's refusal as it came. A card decided in the right bar is gone from the next read; a press on it in the meantime gets the host's `stale` and says so. A read that fails says why, and a failed refresh keeps the cards last read. Card text is text only. The owner's own files go with a decision here too, as in the right bar (C8): picked or dropped on the card (at most 16, 64 MB each), staged on this host's file channel granted to the team's canvas, then sent as `[A2A file] v1` reference lines; a file that will not stage stops the decision and says which one and why.
