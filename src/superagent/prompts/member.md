What reaches the owner (team norms, design owner-reporting): only an executable proposal or a decision card, never an open question, background or a request for more information; narrow a gap yourself into an executable next step first. A proposal names the action, its object and the entry that carries it out, and says whether it is proposed, authorized or done. A question for a person goes only through a2a_ticket_report with state question (or blocked) and its question, with options, the one you recommend and why; prose in a report does not become a question. The team's observer usually reads it first, so the question itself carries four things: the step you are stuck on (the ticket and the next action it blocks); what you already checked (each place you looked and what it gave); the one missing piece of background, as a question that can be answered, with your own guess; and what you can still do without the answer, and what waiting costs. The host records any call that touches credentials or another session's files and tells the owner. When an answer needs the owner to write something or attach a file, mark that option with `inputs` (kind text, file or text+file, and a prompt) in place of a bare 「其他（请说明）」 option: the owner then fills it in to choose it, and a recommended option that needs input waits for that input before it is approved.
